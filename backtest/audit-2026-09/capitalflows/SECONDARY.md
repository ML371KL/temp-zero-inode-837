## 2. Analytical framework (as stated by him or summarized by others)

### What "regime" means to him
- Three-driver model from the first article (2023-01-19): markets are governed by **growth, inflation and liquidity**; a portfolio is "the sum of decisions that are supposed to have durability through all periods of time" (https://www.capitalflowsresearch.com/p/how-to-think-about-markets-trading).
- "Model for Markets" (2023-04-02): regime = cyclical position of growth/inflation, which sets the distribution of outcomes for data and assets; "correctly establish the skew of growth and inflation, you can then correctly analyze when and how much duration risk and credit risk are occurring"; 2022 bear market "was valuation contraction, NOT earnings revisions" (liquidity), "best trades are identifying points where markets price something incredibly unrealistic and fading it" (https://www.capitalflowsresearch.com/p/model-for-markets).
- Four-pillar research stack (2023-03-25): country profile → economic data (flows, labor, prices, capital structure; Integrated Macroeconomic Accounts) → financial-market fundamentals → positioning & microstructure. "The whole world is simply nothing more than a flow chart of capital!" (https://www.capitalflowsresearch.com/p/research-drop-framework).
- Rates first: "Everything in global macro starts with interest rates. They are the price of money that the entire economy and market turn on" (2026-07-10, https://www.capitalflowsresearch.com/p/i-am-going-to-lay-out-my-trading). On the long end: view = short-end/Fed-path view + term premium tied to nominal GDP (Forward Guidance 2024 transcript).
- Since 2025 the dominant layer is **cross-border flows / balance of payments**: "the biggest factor to get right right now is how public market liquidity is shifting due to cross-border flows and trade. I think that's even more important than the Fed"; RRP/TGA plumbing "kind of arbitrary at this point… matters for short end liquidity"; foreigners' record ownership of US assets + record current-account deficit = carry-trade fragility (Forward Guidance 2025-11-19, `yt/20251119_forward-guidance-cross-border-flows.txt`, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eqBnbLU3rNw; "Macro End Game With Global Liquidity" 2025-08-07 cites Klein & Pettis, *Trade Wars Are Class Wars*, https://www.capitalflowsresearch.com/p/macro-end-game-with-global-liquidity; "American Exceptionalism Trade" 2026-07-14: "Capital flows drive trade flows, not the reverse", https://www.capitalflowsresearch.com/p/the-american-exceptionalism-trade).
- Credit cycle / "release valve": "When there is a surplus of money in the system compared to assets, these assets function as 'release valves'" (2023-05-07, https://www.capitalflowsresearch.com/p/macro-reportinsights-liquidity-gold); Bitcoin = "Macro Liquidity Release Valve", a risk-on liquidity play, not a safe haven (primer 2024-04-25; https://www.capitalflowsresearch.com/p/macro-flows-and-bitcoin). 2025–26 thesis: "credit cycle melt-up" with falling real rates; "Seventy to eighty percent of all market returns are driven by macro" (2026-04-14, https://www.capitalflowsresearch.com/p/my-largest-bets-in-the-credit-cycle).

### Indicators he emphasizes
- Growth/inflation: nominal GDP (e.g., "6.5%… explaining why long-term rates have been rising", 2026-08-24, https://www.capitalflowsresearch.com/p/melt-up-or-melt-down-into-end-of), CPI/PPI/NFP/PCE catalysts, weekly claims, real vs nominal consumption, economic-surprise and MoM/3m trends, delinquencies.
- Rates: SOFR curve pricing vs his own path, 2y nominal, inflation swaps, real rates ("Real interest rates will be the most important factor for if Bitcoin begins to make a bottom", 2025-11-24, https://www.capitalflowsresearch.com/p/where-are-we-in-the-liquidity-cycle), curve shape, rate vol compression/expansion, Japan real rates by tenor (SOFR livestream 2026-07-11, `yt/20260711_...`).
- Liquidity/credit: credit spreads vs duration moves, corporate issuance volumes, financial-sector price action, cross-border flows, dollar.
- Positioning/microstructure (2023-04-03 primer): COT (long-term trend, 3m trend, weekly change, z-scores), opex calendar, OI/volume, gamma, IV premium vs realized, term structure, skew, ETF/mutual fund flows, foreign flows, VWAP imbalances, Globex session volumes, seasonality "only if you know the WHY" (https://www.capitalflowsresearch.com/p/positioning-and-microstructure). 2026 "seven forces" from fastest/mechanical to slowest/durable: inelastic markets/passive, mechanical flows, dealer gamma & 0DTE, who trades, cross-border flows, order book, reading liquidity — "One dollar of buying can move the market by five"; decide "whether to fade it or to follow it" (2026-06-23, https://www.capitalflowsresearch.com/p/systematic-flows-and-market-microstructure, `yt/20260623_...`).
- "Positioning premiums" and "asymmetrical information" as alpha sources (Macro Alpha Primer series, 2024).
- Cross-asset confirmation: e.g., CADJPY, crude, ZN, ES used to validate bias (2026-07-10 post).
